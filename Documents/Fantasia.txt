Quests, Titles, and Dungeons (God of LitRPG Box)
Playing vanilla WorldBox is fun, but most of our control over the world is purely physical. We can spawn things, destroy things, and influence civilizations, but we cannot control the system that governs their growth.

This mod suggestion would introduce a LitRPG-style system, allowing players to act as true gods of the system, not just the world itself.


---

Levels
Introduce a leveling system for NPCs.

Features could include:

NPCs gain experience (XP) through actions.

Players can decide the XP required for each level.

Ability to set level caps for certain species or individuals.

Optional control over how fast or slow leveling happens.


This would allow civilizations to naturally grow stronger over time.


---

Stats
Each time an NPC levels up, they gain 3 stat points that can be distributed automatically between common RPG attributes, such as:

Strength

Agility

Endurance

Intelligence

Luck (optional)


These stats would influence combat ability, survival, and productivity.


---
Quests
Okay, this needed to be writen separate, this would allow the player (the god) to create quests for NPCs.

Instead of writing quests manually, players would build them using preset quest components.

Example:

Quest: Rat Killer

Objective:

[Kill] – [10] – [Rats]


Reward:

50 XP


Cooldown:

1 year



---

Quest: Tend the Fields

Objective:

[Plant] – [50] – [Crops]


Reward:

10 XP


Cooldown:

10 years



---

Quests could be assigned to:

The entire world

A specific kingdom

A specific individual


This system would allow players to guide the development of their civilizations through effort-based progression.


---

Titles
Titles would act as permanent achievements for NPCs.

Unlike quests:

Titles cannot be repeated

They have specific requirements

They grant special rewards


Titles would appear as icons on characters, making it easy to identify exceptional individuals.

Example:

Title: Prodigy

Requirements:

Name: Optional (for specific individuals)

Race: None

Age: 1–10

Level: 10

Required Titles: None

Required Traits: None


Rewards:

1000 XP

Trait: Blessed



---

Title: Demigod

Requirements:

Level: 100

Required Title: Prodigy


Rewards:

Multiple traits or large stat bonuses



---

Dungeons
Introduce special locations where NPCs can fight monsters and gain XP.

Possible dungeon types:

Classic dungeons

Towers (similar to manhwa-style tower systems)

Random portals that spawn monsters

Periodic world invasions


Players could control:

Where dungeons spawn

How often they appear

The difficulty and monster types


These areas would act as major sources of experience and adventure for NPCs.


---

Conclusion

This mod would allow players to control not only the world, but also the system behind it.

Instead of just shaping geography and civilizations, players could:

Create progression systems

Assign quests

Reward exceptional individuals

Generate challenges like dungeons
