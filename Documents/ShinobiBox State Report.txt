ShinobiBox: Reborn - State of the Mod (Step A) and Plan (Step B)
=================================================================
Written 2026-10-11 after reading every .cs file in Mods/ShinobiBox/Code, the Locales, the GameResources folder,
your Documents notes, the old Code/prompt.txt, and the core of JinchurikiBox REANIMATED.
Game behaviour was checked against Documents/EntireGameCode.txt (the decompiled game; Worldbox_code/ is empty).
I have NOT played the mod. Anything marked [VERIFY] is strongly suggested by the code but worth seeing in game.


-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. SHORT VERSION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- The mod has a lot of real, good material: ~90 icons, ~25 effect animations, 12 spawnable characters with sprites,
  12 items, 7 sounds, and several genuinely good mechanics (listed in section 2).
- The previous session already fixed most of the Design Review's headline bugs (Mangekyo coin flip, clan birth rules,
  Infinite Tsukuyomi condition, Six Paths roll order, Lee clan hookup, rank-progression typos, Akimichi chakra order).
- But there is a second layer of silent problems the review did not catch. The worst one: several status effects deal
  damage EVERY FRAME instead of every second (section 3, item 1). That makes Amaterasu, Kaiten and Adamantite Chains
  instant kills whose strength depends on your FPS.
- The deeper design problems are still there: abilities are random rolls behind one shared 7-second cooldown,
  big eyes give permanent stats instead of being "activated", two chakra bars do nothing, and power stacks with no budget.
- My recommendation is NOT a rewrite. It is: safety net -> stop-the-bleeding bug pass -> chakra foundation ->
  a small jutsu framework (the big player-visible win) -> finish Uchiha and Nine-Tails to full quality -> then the rest.


-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT IS SOLID (keep these)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- Sharingan 1-3 tomoe as COMBAT STATUSES (only on while fighting, drains chakra). This is the right model for every eye.
- Mangekyo blindness counter (25 uses -> blind), shown in the unit window.
- Kill-to-claim rewards: kill Kurama -> Jinchuriki, kill Madara -> his EMS, kill Sasuke -> his Rinnegan, kill Hashirama -> cells.
- Will of Fire / Curse of Hatred: dying buffs or curses your village. Simple, readable, emotional.
- Indra / Asura reincarnation on death (TransmigrateAction). A great story hook.
- Nine-Tails form ladder with a world log for each first-time form, and Baryon Mode as a "last stand" on death.
- Shadow Clone: copies traits and items, shares chakra, poofs with smoke and sound. The most polished jutsu.
- Rank-based chakra cost discount (Academy pays more, Kage pays less). A real reason ranks matter.
- Config toggles exist and work (clan names, auto ranks, auto favorite, world tips).
- Auto-favorite respects the player un-favoriting a unit. That is exactly the right pattern for every auto-give.

3. SILENTLY BROKEN (verified in the current code)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. DAMAGE EVERY FRAME. In vanilla WorldBox a status "action" with no action_interval runs every frame.
   - Amaterasu: 10 fire damage per frame for 60 s (about 600 damage/s at 60 FPS). Landing it = death.   ShinobiStatus.cs 416
   - Kaiten (Palm Rotation): 40 damage per frame to every non-ally in the chunk for 4 s.               ShinobiStatus.cs 611
   - Adamantite Chains: 5% of max HP per frame for 5 s. Kills anything, including Kurama, in well under a second. ShinobiStatus.cs 1220
   Faster PCs = stronger jutsu. Fix: give these statuses an action_interval and retune.
2. SUSANOO LASTS 1 SECOND. status_base_susanoo, status_perfect_susanoo and madara_ps have duration 1 s, so Susanoo
   is a 1-second flash (+500 / +2000 health for one second), not a form. Only the 10 s "ribcage" lasts.
3. EIGHT GATES FLIP-FLOP AND COST NOTHING. A level-8 unit opens Gate 8; the next proc removes it and opens Gate 7;
   the next one goes back to 8, and so on. InnerGatesStrain is never hooked up and eight_gate_strain is never applied,
   so the gates have no drawback except a 25% chance of PERMANENT crippling at Gate 5.
4. AKATSUKI NEVER RECRUITS. The "evil, age 28+, 1% every 20 s" check lives on the Akatsuki trait itself, so it only
   runs on units that are already Akatsuki. Nobody new ever joins.
5. MANGEKYO HAS NO "ACTIVE" STATE. status_mangekyo exists and has a drain, but nothing ever applies it. Mangekyo, EMS,
   Rinnegan and Byakugan give their big stats permanently, unlike the Sharingan.
6. TWO CHAKRA BARS THAT DO NOTHING. Tailed-Beast Chakra and Sage Chakra regenerate and are displayed, but nothing
   in the mod ever spends them. Every other unit shows "0/0" for both.
7. EVERY UNIT IS STUN-IMMUNE. Main.cs removes "stunned" every 0.1 s from any unit with chakra. Because chakra is
   level x 42, that is almost every unit in the world, animals included. (Maybe intentional, see questions.)
8. ANIMALS HAVE CHAKRA. IsChakraUser never checks isSapient(), so chickens get birth chakra, level chakra and the
   regen/drain loop every 0.1 s.
9. CHAKRA EXHAUSTION LOCK (Design Review B8) is still there: regen is fully blocked while exhausted, and exhaustion
   re-applies as soon as it ends because chakra is still under 15%. The "x0.35 regen while exhausted" line is dead code.
10. PERMANENT CRIPPLING (B12) is still there: Armor Break adds the vanilla "crippled" trait on a 5% proc.
11. AUTO-GIVE LOOPS. If a player removes these by hand, they come back within 3 s: Rinnegan, Sharingan, Sannin,
    Ghost of Uchiha, God of Shinobi, Rinne Sharingan, Six Paths Senjutsu. (Roadmap rule 5 says removal must stick.)
12. TYPOS AND MISSING ASSETS (found by an id/asset cross-check script):
    - Rasenshuriken end effect "fx_shinobiantimatter" (registered as "fx_shinobi_antimatter") -> no explosion.
    - Paper Bomb Kunai end effect "fx_explosion" does not exist in the game -> no explosion.
    - World-log / UI icons that don't exist: ui/icons/Seal, ui/icons/hashirama, ui/icons/nine_tails, ui/icons/ten_tails.
    - trait_akatsuki and trait_blind have no name/description text.
    - 4 locale keys used in code but missing from en.json (status byakugan, status ketsuryuganEHT).
    - Code still checks for "status_jinchuriki_avatar", "jugo_transformation", "one_eyed" and five release traits
      (ice, lava, boil, dust) that do not exist.
13. SOUND PATH IS HARD-CODED to "<game>/Mods/ShinobiBox/GameResources/Sounds/". If the mod folder has any other name
    (Workshop copy, renamed zip), every custom sound silently fails.
14. DESCRIPTIONS DON'T MATCH THE CODE. Examples: Lee clan says "age 24 / level 4", code is age 18 / level 3.
    Nine-Tails says "below 80% HP", code has no HP check. Mangekyo says "2 or 3 tomoe", code needs active 3 tomoe.
    Taijutsu Master says "stats scale", they don't. Frog/Slug/Snake Sage say "No way to obtain this yet".
15. OTSUTSUKI SNOWBALL [VERIFY]. Every Otsutsuki baby gets Hagoromo or Hamura chakra (100%) and an eye (100%).
    Hagoromo chakra = permanent Six Paths Senjutsu (flight, truth-seeking orbs) and Rinnegan at age 40, and ALL children
    of a Hagoromo-chakra parent get Indra or Asura chakra. Otsutsuki clan inherits at 100%. One family can fill a world
    with Rinnegan users.
16. PERFORMANCE [VERIFY in a big world]. Every 0.1 s, for every unit, max chakra is recalculated about 6 times
    (each with a try/catch stat lookup), plus three more full passes over all units on other timers.
    The unit window also recomputes everything every frame.
17. NerfFireDamage.cs changes fire damage for the WHOLE game, not just shinobi. Worth a conscious decision.

6. MY HONEST OPINION: THE BIG DESIGN PROBLEMS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A. Abilities are lottery tickets.
   Almost every jutsu is "on attack or on hit: roll 1-10% -> spend chakra -> add a status". All of them share ONE
   7-second lockout, so whichever jutsu wins the roll blocks the others. Players can't tell why anything happened.
   Fix: a small jutsu framework. Each jutsu is one definition (cost, cooldown, range, a situational condition such as
   "I'm below 40% HP", "3+ enemies near", "target is stronger than me", its sound, effect and first-use world log).
   Each unit keeps its OWN cooldown per jutsu (stored as world time, so it respects pause and game speed).
   A unit in combat simply uses the best available jutsu. JinchurikiBox has the right idea (a Jutsu class with cost,
   cooldown, cast time, required level), but its cooldown is shared by every unit in the world and runs on real time,
   so I'd borrow the idea, not the code.

B. Two kinds of power that fight each other.
   The Sharingan is a trait you own plus a status you activate (drains chakra). Everything else (Mangekyo, EMS, Rinnegan,
   Byakugan, Tenseigan) just gives permanent stats. One rule for every eye and mode: the TRAIT is what you own, the
   STATUS is what's switched on in combat, and only the status gives the big stats and drains chakra.
   JinchurikiBox does it this way too, and it makes fights visibly "power up".

C. Everyone is special, so nobody is.
   Natures roll 30% each at birth (plus the vanilla birth pool and 40% inheritance), ~4% every 3 s turns gifted adults
   into Academy Students, kings become Kage, and stacked bonuses have no budget (characters spawn at level 100 with
   1,000-10,000 base HP before any traits). The scaling guide numbers aren't enforced anywhere.
   I'd rather make shinobi a talented minority, bloodline powers rare, and clamp stacking with a simple budget
   (one "power tier" per unit that decides how much its traits can add). This is my call to make under the brief,
   but it changes how worlds feel, so I want your answer to question 1 first.

D. The named characters are monsters, not people.
   Every character is cloned from "$mob$" with a "random_move" job and a wild kingdom. Naruto, Kakashi and Sakura sit
   in a fake "Konoha" WILD kingdom; they never join villages, marry or have kids. Your old prompt asked for heroes to
   live in villages and villains to be rogues. That's the right goal; it isn't built yet.

E. The code makes typos invisible.
   There are ~400 hand-typed id strings. A typo never errors, the feature just never happens (sections 3.12, 3.4).
   One Ids file with constants plus a startup self-check turns these into errors you see immediately.

F. Big files are mostly copy-paste.
   ShinobiStatus.cs (2,290 lines), ShinobiTraits.cs (2,045) and ShinobiActors.cs (1,375) are mostly repeated blocks.
   Small helpers (one for "an aura status with a texture", one for "a named character") would cut thousands of lines
   without making anything clever. Smaller files = less dread when you open them.

What I'd cut or park (pending your answers):
- Park: Yang / Yin-Yang (no behaviour), Golden Byakugan and Hamura's chakra (copies of other traits), Akatsuki trait
  (until there is an Akatsuki event), Ten-Tails progression beyond "kill the Juubi".
- Rebuild small or cut: Sage modes. If rebuilt: Toad, Slug, Snake (and Wood for Hashirama), fuelled by the sage chakra
  bar, with a clear way to become a sage. JinchurikiBox's version (activate when chakra >= 60%, burns nature energy
  per second, Toad sage turns into a frog if they mess up) is fun, but it has NO natural way to obtain sage mode either.
- Keep and finish: chakra, ranks, Uchiha line, Nine-Tails, Hyuga, Uzumaki, the named characters.

7. RANKED PLAN (Step C), each step is its own small update
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Scope: S = a few hours of work and a handful of commits. M = a focused session. L = more than one session.

0. Compile check (S). There is no .NET SDK here, but the needed DLLs are in NeoModLoaderWORKING/assembly-dependencies.
   I'll install the SDK in this environment and build against them, so every commit at least compiles.
   The game itself still has to be tested by you.
1. Safety net (S). Ids.cs with constants for every trait/status/effect/projectile/item id, used everywhere, plus a
   startup self-check that logs every missing id, locale key and icon. Fix everything it finds (section 3.12).
   No ids change, so saves are unaffected.
2. Stop-the-bleeding bug pass (M). Per-frame damage, Susanoo durations, gate flip-flop plus strain, Akatsuki recruiting,
   exhaustion lock, crippling -> timed status, auto-give loops respect manual removal, descriptions rewritten to
   match the code with exact numbers. Players feel this immediately.
3. Chakra foundation (M). Only sapient units get chakra; max chakra cached once per tick; exhaustion slows regen and
   ends at a set % instead of locking; Tailed-Beast chakra fuels Nine-Tails forms and the Tailed Beast Bomb; Sage chakra
   fuels sage modes; a chakra bar under the health bar (like JinchurikiBox) instead of three icons showing 0/0.
   One pass over units instead of four.
4. Jutsu framework + ability feel (L). The biggest visible change. All existing jutsu move into it, the global 7 s
   lockout goes, each jutsu gets a purpose/condition, per-unit cooldown, and its own feedback.
5. Uchiha line to full quality (M), as the quality bar: Sharingan 1-3 -> Mangekyo (active status, drain, blindness)
   -> EMS -> Rinnegan path. Amaterasu becomes scary but not frame-rate death; Susanoo becomes a real form.
6. Nine-Tails chain finished (M): forms use tailed-beast chakra; Avatar finished or removed; Baryon verified; Kurama/Juubi
   behaviour sane (right now Kurama itself goes into KCM forms when hit).
7. Sage modes rebuilt small, or cut (M), per your answer.
8. Characters and kingdoms (M): heroes in villages, villains as rogues, one short definition per character
   (ShinobiActors.cs shrinks by roughly 70%), sane spawn levels.
9. Cleanup pass, ongoing alongside the others (S-M): dead code removed, duplicates merged, en.json synced,
   other languages flagged, new config toggles.
10. Balance pass with a real stat budget, then your "later" ideas (summons, Akatsuki/invasions, more beasts).

Every step ends with: a commit series on claude/sleepy-euler-hegpj5, a plain-language entry in
Documents/ShinobiBoxUpdateLog.txt (what changed / why / what to test), and an in-game test checklist.
