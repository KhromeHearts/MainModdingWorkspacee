ShinobiBox Rework - Patch Notes (work in progress, not released yet)
=====================================================================
Branch: claude/sleepy-euler-hegpj5. Every entry says what changed, why, and what to test in game.
[NEEDS IN-GAME TEST] = compiles and was checked against the game code, but nobody has watched it in a world yet.


PART 1 - BROKEN IDS AND MISSING ASSETS
--------------------------------------
- Rasenshuriken and Paper Bomb Kunai had no explosion (their effect ids didn't exist).
- Missing icons: tailed-beast chakra, the Hashirama Cells birth log, the Kurama/Juubi sealed logs.
- Blind and Akatsuki showed raw text keys instead of their names.
- Missing text for the Palm Rotation cooldown and Exploding Human Technique.
- Code checked for traits that don't exist (ice/lava/boil/dust release, Jugo, one_eyed, the cut Avatar form).
- Natures renamed to match the other releases: fire_release, water_release, earth_release, wind_release,
  lightning_release, storm_release (were fireN, waterN, ..., stormR). Old saves lose those traits.

PART 2 - BUG FIXES
------------------
- Damage every frame. In WorldBox a status "action" with no interval runs EVERY FRAME, so these were instant kills
  that got stronger on faster PCs:
  - Amaterasu: was ~600 damage per second for 60 s. Now 3% max health + 6 per second for 12 s (about 40% of a
    unit's health), -40% speed, -15 armor. Still burns fire-proof units. Strong but survivable.
  - Palm Rotation (Kaiten): hit every enemy in a huge area every frame. Now 40 damage every 0.5 s, only within 3 tiles.
  - Adamantine Chains: killed even Kurama in under a second. Now 15% max health on grab (max 250, healing the
    Uzumaki) then 3% per second (max 40).
  - Kurama's Chakra Arm Swipe hit a huge area; now 3 tiles around the target.
- Eight Inner Gates:
  - Gates now open one by one (1, 2, 3...), up to one gate per level. A level 8 unit used to flip between gate 8
    and gate 7 on every proc.
  - Gates 4-8 now hurt the user every second (0.5% to 2.5% of max health). This code existed but was never used.
  - When gate 5+ closes on its own: 30 s of Eight Gates Strain (weaker, can't reopen gates).
    The Gate of Death lasts 30 s and leaves the user at 10% health with 90 s of strain, plus a world log.
  - No more 25% chance of permanent crippling at gate 5. Instead, the Gate of Death (gate 8) cripples for life
    when it closes, like Might Guy. Hashirama Cells or other healing can still cure it.
- Chakra exhaustion could lock a unit for a very long time. Now it triggers when you spend your last 10% of chakra,
  chakra still regenerates (slowly) while exhausted, and when it ends you catch a second wind (25% chakra).
- Armor Break (Taijutsu / Ten-Tails) no longer gives the permanent "crippled" trait. It's now -25 armor and
  -20% speed for 10 seconds.
- Removed traits stay removed: Rinnegan, Sannin, Ghost of Uchiha, God of Shinobi, Rinne Sharingan, Mangekyo and
  Sharingan used to come back 3 seconds after you removed them by hand.
- The Akatsuki never recruited anyone (the check only ran on existing members). Now evil units aged 28+ and level
  5+ can be recruited, never more than 10 living members, with a world log.
- Stun immunity (your AntiStun idea) now covers every unit, not just chakra users.
- Sounds now work from the Steam Workshop or a renamed GameBanana folder (the path used to be hard-coded).
- The fire nerf stays, but if your standalone Nerf Fire Damage mod is also installed, ShinobiBox turns its own copy
  off so burning isn't changed twice.
- Descriptions now state the real rules: Lee clan, Nine-Tails, Mangekyo, Taijutsu Master, Rinne Sharingan,
  Akatsuki, Eight Gates, and every text that still mentioned the removed Nature Exp system.

NOT CHANGED YET (on purpose)
- Susanoo still only lasts 1 second (it's a flash, not a form). That's a design change, so it comes with the
  Uchiha rework instead of being patched blindly now.

PART 3 - SAGE MODE
------------------
- Becoming a sage: units level 6+, age 16+, with Greater or Vast chakra reserves (or Senju with Wood Release) get
  one 3% roll per in-game year to become a Sage Apprentice (new trait, world log). The animal is random
  (toad, slug or snake); Senju wood users always train Wood Sage Mode.
- Training: apprentices meditate when they aren't fighting (the meditating animation). Being hit breaks it.
  5 finished meditations = sage. 35% become perfect sages; imperfect sages burn sage chakra twice as fast.
  Toad apprentices have a 1.5% chance per meditation to take in too much natural energy and turn into a toad.
  Removing Sage Apprentice by hand cancels the training for good.
- Sage Mode: switches on in combat when sage chakra is at least half full, and lasts until it runs out
  (300 sage chakra, 10 per second, imperfect 20). Sage chakra refills slowly outside of fights and fast while
  meditating, and sages meditate on their own when they're low.
- The big stats moved from the sage traits to the Sage Mode statuses, so sages are only that strong while the
  mode is on. Signature moves (cost 15 sage chakra):
  - Toad: Frog Kumite, an extra hit that knocks the enemy back.
  - Slug: Strength of a Hundred Healings when the mode starts (3% max health + 2 per level, every second, 20 s).
  - Snake: regenerates 2% health per second; bites can poison.
  - Wood: roots the target and enemies within 3 tiles in wood.
- Characters who already have sage traits (Naruto, Sakura, Orochimaru, Hashirama) are perfect sages.

PART 4 - CHAKRA FOUNDATION
--------------------------
- Who has chakra: sapient units, ShinobiBox characters and the tailed beasts. Animals don't anymore.
- Named characters (Naruto, Madara...) have no subspecies, so WorldBox never counted them as "sapient" and some
  rules skipped them. They now count as shinobi: e.g. Madara killing Kurama makes him its jinchuriki, sages like
  Naruto meditate, and Sannin can be given to them.
- Performance: max chakra is calculated once per unit per tick (it was ~6 times), and the four separate loops over
  every unit are now one.
- Tailed-beast chakra finally does something:
  - Nine-Tails forms burn it every second (V2 and Incomplete Beast burn fastest, Kurama Link Mode the slowest)
    and end when it runs out. A jinchuriki needs a quarter of it to transform.
  - It refills while not transformed. Normal chakra keeps regenerating during forms now.
  - Tailed Beast Bombs (140) and the Ten-Tails blast (70) cost tailed-beast chakra instead of normal chakra.
  - Kurama (2000) and the Juubi (4000) have much bigger pools than their jinchuriki (500 / 1000).
  - Kurama no longer goes into jinchuriki cloak forms itself.
- Birth talent (the "middle ground" world): the chakra reserve a unit is born with is its talent.
  Vast 3%, Greater 7%, High 15%, Low 20%, none 55%. Clan babies always get at least Low.
  - Natures depend on talent: first nature 50 / 70 / 90 / 100% by tier, extra natures much rarer (4+ is possible
    but very rare). Ordinary people don't roll natures (but can still inherit them from their parents).
  - Storm Release: 25% for units born with both Lightning and Water. Yin/Yang: 3% for Greater/Vast talent.
  - Babies are no longer born as Academy Students; talented units enroll as they grow up.
  - Birth traits now roll after a baby inherits from its parents (before, clan babies rolled before they even had
    their clan).
- Unit window: Tailed-Beast and Sage chakra only show for units that have them (no more 0/0 on everyone), and
  Blindness only shows for Mangekyo users.

TEST CHECKLIST [NEEDS IN-GAME TEST]
1. Spawn Naruto and let him fight. When hit he should enter Frog Sage Mode, the Sage Chakra number in his window
   should go down, and the mode should end when it hits 0. Out of combat he should sometimes meditate.
2. Amaterasu: spawn Itachi near a village and let him fight. A burning unit should lose about 40% health over
   12 seconds, not die instantly. Check that the black-flame animation still shows.
3. Palm Rotation: give a unit Byakugan (level 3+) and let it fight a group. Only enemies right next to it
   should take damage.
4. Uzumaki: let an Uzumaki fight Kurama. Kurama should NOT die from the chains.
5. Eight Gates: give a level 8 unit the Eight Inner Gates trait and let it get hurt. The gate icon should go up
   1 -> 2 -> 3..., health should tick down from gate 4, and when gate 5+ times out the unit gets
   "Eight Gates Strain". Gate 8 should post a world log and leave the unit at 10% health and crippled when it ends.
6. Exhaustion: give a unit Mangekyo and watch it fight until it runs low on chakra. "Chakra Exhaustion" should
   appear and go away after about 10 seconds, with chakra jumping to 25%.
7. Remove a unit's Sharingan by hand. Wait 10 seconds. It should NOT come back.
8. Akatsuki: in a world with evil units, let a few years pass. Someone should join (world log), and there should
   never be more than 10 living members.
9. Sounds: Kamui / Shadow Clone / Amenotejikara should still make their sound.
10. If you have the Nerf Fire Damage mod installed, the log should say ShinobiBox's fire nerf was turned off.
11. Give a few level 6+ units "Vast Chakra Reserve" and run the world for some years. A few should become Sage
    Apprentices (world log), meditate, and eventually become sages. Hit an apprentice while it meditates: the
    meditation should stop.
12. Give a unit Fire Release, Water Release, etc. from the trait menu: names and descriptions should show properly.
13. Kurama: spawn Kurama and let a level 5+ unit kill it. The killer gets Nine Tails Jinchuriki. In fights it
    should go into cloak forms, the Tailed-Beast Chakra number should go down, and the form should end at 0.
    Kurama itself should NOT get a cloak aura.
14. New world: after ~20-30 years, roughly a third to half of the people should be ranked shinobi, most with 0-1
    natures, a few with 2-3. Tell me if it feels like too many or too few.
15. Click a normal villager and an animal: no "0/0" tailed-beast or sage chakra icons. Animals show no chakra.
16. Old save: existing units keep their traits (they are not re-rolled).
